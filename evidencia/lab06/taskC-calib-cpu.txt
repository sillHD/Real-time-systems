Week 6 Task C: calib, duration and the console thread's own CPU time
ESP32-C6, firmware/kernel, 2026-10-21. Three runs of `calib` per build, console output:
"calibration done (<duration> ms, CPU <us> us)". CPU comes from the kernel's
per-thread runtime counters (k_thread_runtime_stats_get on the console thread,
on since week 4 because CONFIG_THREAD_ANALYZER selects them).

calibration done (500 ms, CPU 10583 us)
calibration done (500 ms, CPU 10583 us)

Reading:
- before: CPU = 98.2 % of the duration; the other 7 ms are the threads above
  the console preempting it. The analyzer agrees: console pulse 403.457 ms.
- k_usleep(400) rounds up to whole ticks plus one: 2 ticks = 2 ms at 1 kHz,
  5 ticks = 0.5 ms at 10 kHz. 1000 settles: 2000 ms (misses the 1 s deadline
  on duration alone) or 500 ms (fits).
- after: 10.6 us of CPU per settle (read + sleep + two switches), 1000 x =
  10.6 ms, against 396 ms spinning.
